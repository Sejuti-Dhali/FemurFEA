Experiment 03, Problem 3.1 uses femur geometry with structural steel and Ti-6Al-4V material assignments. The original ANSYS Student results compare total deformation, maximum principal stress and equivalent (von Mises) stress under 550 N and 1050 N loads.

At both tested loads, the titanium alloy case has greater reported deformation than structural steel, while equivalent stress is similar.

Source and limitations
